NEW TO MONOATOMIC MINERALS?

M-State - ORMUS - White Powder Gold

Monoatomic (M-State) minerals are elements that exist as single individual atoms rather than in the usual clustered bonds found in standard minerals.

Because they are in a monoatomic state, they lack the typical standard metallic properties of the base elements.

The ancient Egyptians mastered the art of altering the state of minerals and metals for spiritual and health benefits.

This altered state allows these minerals to be more "bio-available" or easier for the body to interact with, compared to traditional mineral forms.

Monoatomic minerals are not normally found in regular diet and are engineered for energy, cognition, and long-horizon longevity.

We formulate them into three different forms.

  • Powders
  • Liquids
  • Topical Oils
